Teamworks AMS (Smartabase) is integrated with Rice Lake Bluetooth Physician Scales to collect body weight data through the Teamworks AMS APP

  • To learn more about the partnership and workflow click here
  • If you are interested in the Teamworks AMS Gatorade Weight Tracking Platform click here

Rice Lake Weighing Systems is a global leader in the design and manufacturing of weight-related products and technologies. Rice Lake manufacturers multiple bluetooth enabled physician scales that will connect to Teamworks AMS (according to Rice Lake).

Below are the 3 best options for integrating with Teamworks AMS. Remember, each scale will require an Ipad to capture body weights.

All scales have the same weighing platform, but have different display options.

1. Model 150-10-7

This model is the scale recommended by Teamworks AMS and has been tested. I have personally and successfully used this model with Teamworks AMS.

The scale has a remote display, that is connected to the scale platform by cord. This set up allows for the display to be hidden if desired and for body weight to be collected semi blinded via a workflow within the Teamworks AMS App.

 

2. Model 150-10-8

The newest model in Rice Lake's catalog of scales. This scale offers the best portability, with the newest bluetooth technology.

3. Model 150-10-5

This is an eye level display, with a stadoimeter included (for height measurement). This option is not nearly as portable but does fit better in a clinic style setting.  Through the use of an Ipad you can capture body weight automatically and also record height at the same time.
