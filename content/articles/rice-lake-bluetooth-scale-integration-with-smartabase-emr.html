Smartabase (Teamworks AMS + EMR), an Athlete Management System primarily used within athletic and military organizations developed a bluetooth integration with one of Rice Lake's physician scales. Model 150-10-7 BLE directly connects to Smartabase, via the Teamworks AMS App for seamless body weight tracking.

Scale Details

This particular Rice Lake Scale comes in two models, standard and bluetooth enabled. Regardless of where you decide to purchase your scale, double check that you are ordering the bluetooth enabled model.  The bluetooth model with have "BLE" in the model name: 150-10-7 BLE.

In addition to verifying the bluetooth model, make sure you ask for the AC power adapter to plug the scale into the wall. This is an additional part number.

This scale model does have a remote display, meaning the display is not connected to the scale platform and can be mounted to a wall or hidden inside a kiosk stand.

Bluetooth enabled Rice Lake Scale, Model 150-10-7 BLE, integrates with Smartabase. This is the only scale model that is capable of successfully integrating.
